what's inside

braws is a small, cozy place for you and your closest friends. In the app, your friends are your braws.

pet

a pet that needs you

Pick a little companion and look after it. It reacts to how you're doing and to how much you show up for your friends.

room

a room all yours

Decorate an isometric room with furniture, walls and floors. Your braws can drop by and see it.

stamps

one stamp a day

A photo, a doodle, a mood or a voice note. Over time it becomes a calendar of your days.

world

a shared world

See your braws on a little planet. Showing your city is optional and only ever approximate.

wall & plans

the weekly wall

Share what's going on this week, reply to your braws, and set up a movie night, a hike or a coffee.

gifts

Send a matcha, a chocolate or a note to someone who needs it today.

how it works

braws is invite-only. You can't search for strangers and they can't find you.

  1. get an inviteA friend who already uses braws sends you an invite link or a code.
  2. get the appInstall braws from the App Store, then open the invite link (or type the code).
  3. sign in with ApplePick a username, choose your pet and start your little world together.

safety & privacy

Small on purpose. Read the full privacy policy.

  • 16 and over. braws is not for anyone younger.
  • Sign in with Apple only. No email, no password, no phone number.
  • Private stays private. What's only yours lives on your phone and in your own iCloud, which we can't read.
  • Friends only. What you share is seen by the friends you accepted. No public profiles.
  • Report and block. Anyone, anytime. Reports are reviewed, normally within 24 hours.
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, no push notifications, and we never sell your data.

questions

How do I get an invite?

Ask a friend who already uses braws. In the app they can create an invite and send you the link. There's no waitlist and no public sign-up.

Is braws free?

Yes. braws has no paid features, no in-app purchases and no ads. The coins and items in the app are just for fun and have no money value.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad. braws isn't on Android yet.

Who can see what I post?

Only the friends you accepted. Friends of your friends can see your profile and room, so they can send you a request, but not your posts, stamps or location.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple account with iCloud on and open braws: your pet, room and things come back from your iCloud, and what you shared comes back from our server.

Why do I have to be 16?

braws is built for close friends who are 16 or older. The app checks your birth month and year on your phone only; that date is never sent to us.

Which languages does it speak?

27, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Arabic.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: settings, then delete account. It's erased from our server straight away. No app? See delete your account.
