braw

your world, with your real friends

braw is a small, cozy app for staying close to the people you actually care about. You get a pet to look after, a room to decorate, a stamp a day, little gifts to send, a weekly wall and plans with your braws.

No feed to scroll, no strangers, no follower counts. braw is invite-only: you join with a code from a friend.

braw is in early testing on iPhone.
